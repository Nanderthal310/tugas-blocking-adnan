Tokyo Warehouse & International Carriers

Learn about our shipping partners, packaging standards, and restricted goods.

Supported Shipping Methods
  • DHL Express Worldwide: 2 - 4 business days. Signature required, ultra-fast tracking.
  • FedEx International Priority: 3 - 5 business days. Excellent handling for electronics & figures.
  • Japan Post EMS: 4 - 7 business days. National postal network, no remote area surcharges.
  • Surface Seamail: 30 - 60 days. Best budget solution for heavy parcels & Gunpla hauls.
Prohibited & Restricted Items

In compliance with international aviation regulations, the following cannot be shipped overseas:

  • Flammable liquids, perfumes, room diffusers, nail polish
  • Explosives, lighters, fireworks, aerosol spray cans
  • Standalone lithium-ion battery packs or power banks
  • Perishable fresh foods, meats, or plants requiring quarantine

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about proxy purchasing, Mercari rules, and Tokyo warehouse services.

How does JapaLink proxy shopping work?
Many domestic Japanese online stores (such as Mercari Japan, Booth, Surugaya) do not ship internationally or require Japanese phone numbers and domestic payment cards. JapaLink acts as your personal shopping agent in Tokyo: we purchase the item for you, receive it at our Tokyo warehouse, store it safely, and ship it directly to your address worldwide.
Why are there two payments (1st & 2nd Payment)?
This is the standard, fairest method used by top Japanese proxies: The 1st Payment covers the actual item cost plus our low proxy service fee (¥300 / item). The 2nd Payment covers international shipping, which can only be accurately calculated after your goods arrive at our Tokyo warehouse, are measured, weighed, and consolidated according to your carrier preference.
How long can I store items in your Tokyo warehouse for free?
All packages enjoy 30 days of 100% FREE warehouse storage from the day of arrival! This gives you plenty of time to order from multiple different stores, wait for all packages to arrive, and consolidate them into a single parcel to save up to 70% on international shipping.
Can I buy items from any Japanese website not listed in the menu?
Yes! Use our "Other Store" Custom URL Order feature. Simply copy the link of any product from any Japanese website (e.g. Bandai Namco, Melonbooks, Toranoana, Yahoo Auctions), enter the title and price in JPY, and add it to your cart. Our agents will process it manually.
